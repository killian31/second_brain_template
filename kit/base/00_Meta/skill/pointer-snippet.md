## Second brain (read it and write to it)

The user keeps a knowledge vault, their "second brain", at `$VAULT`. Agents maintain it so that every tool and every session shares the same context. Reading it and writing to it are both part of your job.

- **When you start work**, read `$VAULT/AGENTS.md` (the manual) and scan `$VAULT/INDEX.md` (a catalog of every note with a one-line summary). Search the vault before you assume you lack context about the user, their projects or their past decisions. It is probably already written down.
- **Write back without asking.** When a task produces or reveals something worth keeping (a result, a benchmark, a decision, a fact, a preference, a bug, a new concept), record it as the last step of the task and carry on. Write one idea per note, in about 100 to 400 words, with a `summary:` line in the frontmatter and `[[wikilinks]]` to related notes. Many small linked notes are better than a few large ones. Do not stop to ask "should I save this to the second brain?" Write it, following `AGENTS.md`, and mention that you did. Skip only what is trivial or will not matter tomorrow.
- **Limits.** Never invent facts about the user. If you do not know, leave the field empty. Reading, appending and creating notes need no confirmation. Deleting notes, restructuring folders and rewriting existing notes in bulk need the user's explicit OK.
